# Card prices with insight (022, rules r13): the reasoning

Reasoning first (principles.md, *Calibrate by reasoning first*), then the screen.

**The budget.** A board makes 4 insight a round (022: was 3) plus the presence track's
+1 spaces (most boards reach 5 by round 3-4), and 10 / 9 credits plus buildings. Four
cards are drafted a round. Keeps cost 1 insight (tier 1) or 3 (tier 2), credits buy
keep insight only, at 5:1. So a round's insight is roughly "keep 2, play 2", or "keep
a tier-2 card and play one", and keeping a card you won't play in the next round or two
costs a play now.

**When a card earns its cost (8-10 round games; most wins end in round 6-8).**

- *Income buildings* (Fab Line, Trade Office, Solar Field, Microgrid, Server Farm, Data
  Lake, Holding Company): 3-6 credits for about +1 a round pay back in 3-5 rounds, so
  they are worth it in rounds 1-4 only. **1 insight**: a building is the default.
- *Engine buildings* (Assembly Cluster, Logistics AI, Overclock Rig, Grid Battery,
  Firewall Node, Watchtower Net, Unity Network, Civic Shelter, Recruitment Hub, Field
  Office): value grows with the rounds left. **1 insight**.
- *Removal events* (Precision Strike, Sweep, Strike Team): the purge win is built out of
  them, any round. **1 insight** (a 022 adaptation of "events are 0"): at 0 the cards
  that win the game were free to play once kept, so insight's only real sink was keeps
  and **keep-all beat the engine** (c2 d2, 32 seeds: 91% against 59%). At 1 they compete
  with keeps: keep-all 69% against the engine's 62% at c2 d2, 44% against 62% at c3 d2.
  Pricing them at 1 *without* the base insight raise cut the engine to 75% at d0.
- *Tempo events* (Security Patch, Rally Point, Jam Signals, Contract Bid, Scavenge, Seed
  Funding, Shelter Order): small, one-off. **0 insight** (Matt: events are 0).
- *Small programs* (Data Mining, Fork Process, Recon Sweep, Evacuate, Shore Up, Rally,
  Grant, Power Share, Power Surge, Liquidate Reserves, Venture Capital, Shelter Order):
  one-off effects worth 1-3 credits. **0 insight**.
- *Programs that stay and build* (Emergency Fab, Rebuild, Grassroots Movement, Security
  Cell): **1 insight**.
- *Tier-2 buildings* (Fusion Plant, Quantum Core, Orbital Lance, Antimatter Refinery,
  Megastructure): 7-13 credits plus hardware or antimatter; built in rounds 3-6 they pay
  for 3-5 rounds. **2 insight**, so with the keep (3) a tier-2 building is a round's
  insight: a real commitment. Fusion Plant, Antimatter Refinery and Orbital Lance also
  **lower your credit income by 1** (the Terraforming Mars cost): an energy or antimatter
  engine bought with credit income.
- *Big tier-2 effects* (Purge Protocol, Counter-intelligence, Swarm Override, Global
  Mandate, Reconstruction, Diaspora, Zero-Day Wipe, Node Takedown): **2 insight**.
- *Unique buildings* (Turnkey Factory, Modular Expansion, Point Defense): **1 insight**;
  the starting buildings 0.

Credit prices are 021's. The "count as power" token pattern (a card's strength scaling
with its tokens) was not needed by this repricing and is not built.
